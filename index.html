      How risk is worked out

      • Flooding: PUB has reported a flash flood within your radius.
      • High risk: a rain station within your radius has had a cloudburst, ≥ 5 mm in 5 min (≈ 60 mm/h) or ≥ 25 mm in 30 min (≈ 50 mm/h). Most reported floods in Singapore follow rain this intense.
      • Watch: heavy rain nearby by the Meteorological Service's scale (≥ 10 mm/h: ≥ 1 mm in 5 min or ≥ 5 mm in 30 min), or the 2-hour forecast for your area is heavy or thundery showers.
      • Low: none of the above.

      Flood alert circles show PUB's broadcast radius, not the size of the flood.

      Data: PUB flood alerts, NEA rainfall and 2-hour forecast via data.gov.sg. Refreshes every 2 minutes.

      Not an official warning service. For PUB's own alerts, subscribe via the myENV app or PUB's Telegram channel.
